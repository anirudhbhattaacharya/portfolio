01 · work

What I do on a PD case

I am an Application Engineer. I have not owned a product tapeout. I debug the Fusion Compiler and ICC2 failures a PD team hits when the run or the constraints break.

Timing / CTS

clock_opt, MSCTS crashes, generated clocks, clock mux and gating, NDR on leaf clocks, useful skew, CCD, FC vs ICC2 skew mismatch.

Congestion / route

IP edge congestion, magnet placement, legalizer and poly rules, hyper_route_opt, via ladder, off-grid M2, power domain routing.

Low power

Signoff class
