About Me

I am Anirudh Bhattacharya, a Physical Design Application Engineer at Synopsys in Hyderabad. I started as a PD intern in April 2024, finished Fusion Compiler RTL-to-GDS training, ran DSO.ai on SLM PVT Monitor IP at Intel 18A / IFS18A, and converted to AE in April 2025.
